SimplePOS Live

Billing and stock software for kirana, general and retail shops. Runs in the browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and keeps working when the internet drops.

  • Fast counter billing: barcode scan, hold bills, keyboard shortcuts, UPI QR for the exact amount
  • GST bills, returns, and 58/80 mm or A4 printing; bills shared on WhatsApp
  • Stock that updates with every sale and purchase, with low-stock and expiry alerts
  • Customer credit (udhaar) with limits and reminders
  • Offers that apply by themselves, sales and profit reports, Excel export
  • Staff logins with roles, and more than one shop on one dashboard

DinePOS In development

Order management and billing for restaurants. Captains take orders at the table, the kitchen gets a KOT, and the counter prints the bill.

  • Live table view: free, running and bill printed, with running total and time
  • Orders sent to the kitchen as KOTs; items added later go as a new KOT
  • Dine-in, takeaway and online orders in one place
  • Split bills, move or merge tables, GST and service charge
  • Built on the same offline-first base as SimplePOS

Cake of the Day Android & web

An ordering app that took a cake shop in Ratnagiri and Chiplun online. Customers browse cakes, choose size and quantity, pay online or cash on delivery, and track their order.

  • Login with mobile number and OTP
  • Catalogue by category: cakes, pastries, cookies, snacks, candles and more
  • Size options, a message for the cake, and delivery date and time
  • Razorpay for UPI, cards and net banking, or cash on delivery
  • Built-in chat assistant for common questions
  • Separate menus and orders for each branch; the shop gets an email for every new order

NFC Employee Attendance Hardware + software

Employees tap their ID card on a reader at the entrance. The time is recorded automatically, so there is no register to fill and no buddy punching.

  • Each employee ID card carries an NFC tag linked to that person
  • First tap of the day marks in, the next marks out
  • Late arrivals and missing check-outs are easy to spot
  • Monthly attendance ready for salary calculation
  • Low-cost parts, simple to install at a shop, office or factory gate
Hardware
Arduino, RC522 NFC/RFID reader, NFC ID cards
For
Small offices, workshops and factories

Tiffin Mess Subscriptions Android app

A marketplace for local messes. Each mess lists its weekly menu, prices and meal timings. People nearby subscribe for the month and pick their meals each day during meal hours.

  • Find messes near your address, with veg / non-veg marks and ratings
  • Day-wise menu for breakfast, lunch and dinner, priced per month
  • Subscribe and pay online, with GST and promo codes at checkout
  • Choose today's items only while that meal's ordering window is open
  • Mess owners see incoming orders and move them along: confirmed, preparing, out for delivery
  • Customers rate the order once it arrives; FSSAI certificate shown on each mess
